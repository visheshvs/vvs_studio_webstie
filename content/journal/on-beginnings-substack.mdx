## How to Use This Template

1. Copy this file and rename it to match your Substack post (e.g., `my-latest-thoughts.mdx`)
2. Update the frontmatter:
   - `title`: Your Substack post title
   - `date`: Publication date (format: "YYYY-MM-DD")
   - `excerpt`: A brief description (1-2 sentences) for the preview card
   - `externalLink`: The full URL to your Substack post
3. Save the file in `content/journal/`
4. The post will appear in your journal list with a "Read on Substack ↗" badge
5. Clicking the card will open the Substack post in a new tab
