In Japan, the *ongaku kissa* (music café) and jazz kissaten have long been sanctuaries for serious listeners. These are places where the turntables, amplifiers, and speakers are chosen with care, where the coffee or the alcohol is good, but the sound is the main event.

## What Makes Them Special

Unlike a typical café with background music, these spaces treat listening as the primary activity. Patrons come to hear full albums on high-end systems, often in near-silence. The ritual of flipping a record, the warmth of vinyl, the shared appreciation for a well-mastered pressing, it's a different kind of social space. This ethos matches the vibe of this space/website: to slow down and enjoy art at the center, with a world curated around it.

## My Maps

I've been building a [personal map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GmUNDTNsiubpuESm9) of spots I've visited or want to visit, from Tokyo's legendary jazz kissaten to listening bars in other cities.
