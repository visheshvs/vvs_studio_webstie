The art of digging isn't just about finding records—it's about the hunt, the stories behind the grooves, and the moments when a track you've never heard stops you in your tracks.

## What I'm Listening To

My playlists are a mix of crate-digging finds, late-night discoveries, and sounds that have stuck with me over the years. From jazz and soul to ambient and electronic, the collection grows with every dig.

## The Process

There's something meditative about flipping through bins, reading liner notes, and taking a chance on a cover that speaks to you. Some of the best finds come from records you've never heard of—artists who never made it big but left behind something worth preserving.
