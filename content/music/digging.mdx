Music has been one of the most consistent companions in my life.

Over the years, moving through different phases, meeting new people, living in new places, and being exposed to different cultures has completely changed what I listen to. Every season of life seems to come with its own sound. Some stick. Some fade. Some come back years later and hit even harder.

I like exploring music of all kinds. Genres don’t matter much to me anymore. I’ve gone through phases of indie rock, hip-hop, disco, electronic, old Bollywood, jazz, folk, ambient, and everything in between. The more I listened, the less I felt the need to box things in.

So I started curating playlists.

Partly for myself, so I can always return to a specific feeling or time in my life.  
Partly to share. Because sometimes there’s too much to explain in words, and sharing a song is easier.

I genuinely believe all kinds of music can be good. If the intention is honest, if the art feels real, or if the artist is just undeniably a genius, it deserves a listen and maybe a playlist.
